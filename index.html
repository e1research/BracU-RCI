Step-by-Step Tutorial

How to Use the BracU Research Capacity Index

Follow these 5 straightforward steps to evaluate any faculty member or researcher.

1

Search Researcher on ORCID

Navigate to the official ORCID Registry . Use the top search bar labeled "Search the ORCID registry" and enter the researcher's name or university (e.g. "Brac University").

Search example: "Sadia Hamid Kazi" or "Annajiat Alim Rasel"
2

Copy the 16-Digit ORCID iD

Copy the verified 16-digit ORCID identifier located beneath the researcher's name. This guarantees 100% precision with no name collisions.

0000-0002-1825-0097 Pattern: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
3

Obtain a Free Groq API Key

Log in to the Groq Cloud Console (free via Google or GitHub account). Click "Create API Key" and copy your key.

Format: gsk_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
4 & 5

Paste & Generate RCI Report

Paste the ORCID iD and your Groq API key into our Evaluator tab. Click "Compute RCI & Analyze". The system will harvest verified works from ORCID, resolve abstracts, and deliver an AI-generated supervisor evaluation.

Your key remains in your local browser and is never stored on third-party servers.
Mathematical Specification

The Research Capacity Index (RCI) Formula

The RCI is a calibrated metric between 0.00 and 1.00 combining peer-reviewed productivity, citation velocity, supervisory seniority, and AI semantic assessment.

// Master RCI Formulation

RCI = (0.22 · S_prod) + (0.23 · S_impact) + (0.18 · S_lead) + (0.12 · S_collab) + (0.15 · S_momentum) + (0.10 · S_align)

Where each sub-score S_x is strictly normalized between [0.0, 1.0].

Sub-Score Components & Weights

Component Weight Formulation & Rationale Why it matters for Thesis Students
S_prod (Productivity) 22% 0.7·ln(1+PeerReviewedWorks)/ln(1+70) + 0.3·ln(1+TotalWorks)/ln(1+100) Favors verified peer-reviewed articles and conference proceedings over unvetted preprints.
S_impact (Impact & Venue) 23% 0.7·ln(1+Citations)/ln(1+2000) + 0.3·min(1, (Citations/Works)/30) Indicates that the researcher's published work is actively referenced by global peers.
S_lead (Leadership & Mentorship) 18% min(1, (1.0·FirstAuthors + 1.25·LastAuthors) / EvaluatedWorks) Last-authorship reflects senior supervision/laboratory directorship; first-authorship reflects hands-on craftsmanship.
S_collab (Collaboration Span) 12% Optimized between 2 to 7 co-authors; penalizes extreme hyper-authorship or isolated writing. Supervisors with balanced co-authorship networks expose thesis students to broader research opportunities.
S_momentum (Recency) 15% min(1, RecentWorks_3yr / 5) Filters out dormant supervisors; ensures the researcher has active momentum and current project bandwidth.
S_align (Societal & Trendy AI Score) 10% LLM Semantic Scoring [0.0–1.0] on 3 most recent abstracts against Sustainable Development Goals, healthcare, AI ethics, national development. Aligns student research with high-impact scholarship, national priority areas, and contemporary grant requirements.

Institutional Context

About BracU Research Capacity Index

The BracU Research Capacity Index (BRCI) is an academic initiative conceived to empower students at BRAC University (Dhaka, Bangladesh) in navigating thesis supervisor selection, co-authorship outreach, and faculty research profiling.

Unlike automated tools that suffer from author name collision and preprint contamination, BRCI utilizes a Strict ORCID-First Pipeline: works are directly queried from the verified public ORCID registry, categorized by publication type (journal article, conference paper, preprint), and enriched with DOI-specific abstracts and citation metrics.

Expansion Beyond Brac University

While designed initially for BRAC University students and faculty, BRCI's data infrastructure is built on global open scholarly identifiers (ORCID and OpenAlex). As such, it works universally for researchers from BUET, Dhaka University, NSU, IBA, and international universities worldwide without requiring any software modifications.
